## Release-candidate boundary

- **Functionality defects:** none currently identified in the implementable PLAN scope after the integrated checks. A regression failure reopens this item.
- **External hardware/platform verification:** real Safari, physical mobile and GPU performance, microphone latency and audio output quality, screen-reader use, and live UIUC deployment require access unavailable here. WebKit, emulation, fake capture, automated accessibility, and local static hosting provide the recorded proxy evidence above.
- **Subjective owner review:** judge title/lantern legibility and scene composition at the opening and flight inset; chime balance and caption timing; ease of selecting and adjusting the articulated lantern and its flight path.
- **Optional enhancements outside PLAN:** none required for this release candidate.
